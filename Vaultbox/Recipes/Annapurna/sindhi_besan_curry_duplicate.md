# Sindhi Besan Curry — Duplicate Selection

## Original name
सिंधी बेसन करी

This file is provided separately because you selected recipe #14 as well as recipe #12 in the earlier numbered list. Both entries refer to the same photographed recipe.

## Ingredients
- 5 tablespoons besan (gram flour), sifted
- 1 lemon-sized ball of tamarind
- 3–4 dried mango pieces (aamsul)
- 7–8 okra
- 10–12 cluster beans
- 100 g curd
- 1–2 small carrots
- 2 large potatoes
- Fenugreek seeds, cumin seeds, coriander seeds, asafoetida
- Red and green chillies, curry leaves, coriander, turmeric, salt and oil

## Method
1. Soak the tamarind; boil water, add its pulp, and cook the vegetable pieces.
2. Make a smooth besan slurry with water.
3. Prepare a tempering with oil, fenugreek, cumin and coriander.
4. Add the besan slurry; fry while stirring until thick.
5. Add hot water and the cooked vegetables.
6. Season with turmeric, chilli powder, curry leaves, salt, coriander and green chillies.
7. Add dried mango pieces, bring to a boil and serve hot.
