# Mixed Pulao — Version 2

## Original name
मिश्र पुलाव

## Ingredients
- 1 handful green peas
- 1 handful black-eyed peas
- 1 handful jowar
- 1 handful chickpeas
- 1 handful cowpeas
- 2 cups rice
- 2 medium onions
- 2 tomatoes

### Masala
- 2 teaspoons coriander
- 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
- Garlic cloves
- 2 red chillies
- 7–8 cloves

## Method
1. Soak the pulses and grains separately overnight; cook each separately the next day.
2. Cook the rice in the usual way.
3. Heat oil in a pan and fry the onions until browned. Add the ground masala and fry.
4. Add chopped tomatoes and cook until soft.
5. Add the cooked pulses/grains, salt, chilli powder, turmeric and coriander, then mix.
6. Add rice, mix gently and steam for a short while. Serve hot.
