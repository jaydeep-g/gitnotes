# Nagpuri Vada Bhat

## Original name
नागपुरी वडाभात

## Ingredients
- 3 cups rice
- 1 cup matki (moth beans)
- 2 cups thick yogurt
- 1 cup gram flour (besan)
- 1 cup coriander
- 2 green chillies
- 2 tablespoons crushed garlic
- Salt
- Chilli powder, coriander-cumin powder, garam masala, turmeric, asafoetida and oil for tempering

## Method
1. Wash the rice and soak it for a short time. Cook the rice with a little turmeric.
2. Mix in the yogurt and let the rice become loose/fluffy.
3. Prepare a tempering with oil, asafoetida, mustard seeds and turmeric. Add the thick yogurt, keeping the heat low so that it does not split.
4. Add chilli powder and the ground masala, then add the cooked rice and salt. Mix well and keep aside.
5. Mix besan with enough water to make a thick batter. Add crushed garlic, coriander, green chillies and salt. Make small vadas from the batter and fry until crisp.
6. Serve the vadas with the rice.
