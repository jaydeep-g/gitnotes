# Sindhi Besan Curry

## Original name
सिंधी बेसन करी

## Ingredients
- 5 tablespoons besan (gram flour), sifted
- 1 lemon-sized ball of tamarind
- 3–4 dried mango pieces (aamsul)
- 7–8 okra
- 10–12 cluster beans
- 100 g curd
- 1–2 small carrots
- 2 large potatoes

### Masala
- 2 teaspoons fenugreek seeds
- 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
- 1 teaspoon coriander seeds
- A little asafoetida
- 3–4 red chillies
- 3–4 green chillies
- A little curry leaves
- 1 handful chopped coriander
- A little turmeric
- Salt
- 3–4 tablespoons oil

## Method
1. Soak tamarind in a little water. Boil 1 litre water, add tamarind pulp, and cook the vegetable pieces in it.
2. Mix 2 tablespoons besan in 1 glass water until smooth.
3. Heat oil in a pan. Add fenugreek, cumin and coriander, then make a tempering.
4. Add the besan-water mixture and fry, stirring continuously, until it turns thick.
5. Add hot water and then the cooked vegetables.
6. Add turmeric, red chilli powder, curry leaves, salt, coriander and green chillies. Bring to a boil.
7. Add the dried mango pieces and cook well. Serve hot.
