# Gola Bhat

## Original name
गोळा भात

## Ingredients
- 4 cups rice
- 2 cups gram flour (besan)
- Chilli powder, salt, coriander-cumin powder, garam masala
- Coriander leaves
- Seasoning ingredients

## Method
1. The previous night, mix oil into the gram flour. In the morning, add chilli powder, salt, coriander-cumin powder, garam masala and a little coriander. Mix well and form small balls. Steam the balls in a cooker in a single layer, using a strainer.
2. Cook plain rice as usual. Serve one ball per portion of rice; use 2–4 balls per plate. Crush the balls into the rice while eating.
3. In a large pan, prepare a tempering with oil, asafoetida and mustard seeds. Add broken dried red chillies. Add a little tempering to each serving of rice and serve.

**Note:** The recipe says separate tempering is not necessary if the flour mixture has enough oil; if the cooked rice looks dry, crumble a ball into it and mix.
