# Spiced Barnyard Millet Rice

## Original name
वऱ्याच्या तांदळाचा मसालेभात

## Ingredients
- 2 cups barnyard millet (varai / samak rice)
- 1 cup sour yogurt
- 2 cups matara / peas
- 5–6 green chillies
- 1 lemon
- Salt
- A little coriander
- 1 tablespoon coriander seeds
- 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
- 3–4 cloves
- 1 piece cinnamon
- A little fresh ginger

## Method
1. Dry-roast coriander, cumin, cloves and cinnamon; grind with chillies. Keep this masala aside.
2. Wash the barnyard millet and drain it.
3. Prepare a tempering with oil, asafoetida, mustard seeds and turmeric. Add the peas and sauté.
4. Add the ground chilli masala and sauté.
5. Add the drained millet and sauté well. Add hot water, salt and the sour yogurt. Cover and cook the rice.
6. The rice is ready in about 10 minutes. Garnish with fresh ginger and coriander before serving.

**Serving note:** The book suggests serving it when plain rice is unavailable, or as a variation for fasting-day meals.
