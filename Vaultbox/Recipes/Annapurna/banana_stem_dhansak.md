# Banana Stem Dhansak

## Original name
केळीदेठीचा धनसाक

## Ingredients
- 1 bowl banana stem
- 2 handfuls moong dal
- 1 handful masoor dal
- 1 medium onion
- 2 tomatoes
- 2 green chillies
- 2 tablespoons tamarind pulp

### Masala
- 1 teaspoon fenugreek seeds
- 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
- 1 teaspoon coriander seeds
- 2 tablespoons chilli powder
- 3–4 dried red chillies
- Coriander leaves
- A little turmeric
- Salt
- 3–4 tablespoons oil

## Method
1. Soak tamarind in a little water. Boil 1 litre water and cook the dal and banana-stem pieces in it.
2. Blend 2 tablespoons besan in 1 glass water and make it smooth.
3. Heat oil in a pan. Add fenugreek, cumin and coriander; prepare a tempering.
4. Add the besan-water mixture and fry continuously until it thickens.
5. Add hot water and the cooked dal-and-banana-stem mixture.
6. Add turmeric, chilli powder, coriander, salt and chopped chillies. Bring to a boil.
7. Add tamarind pulp and the vegetable pieces. Cook until hot and serve.
