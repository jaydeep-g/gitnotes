# Mixed Pulao — Version 1

## Original name
मिश्र पुलाव

## Ingredients
- 1 handful green peas
- 1 handful black-eyed peas
- 1 handful jowar
- 1 handful chickpeas
- 1 handful cowpeas
- 2 cups rice
- 2 medium onions
- 2 tomatoes

### Masala
- 2 teaspoons coriander
- 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
- Garlic cloves
- 2 red chillies
- 7–8 cloves

## Method
1. Soak each pulse/grain separately overnight and cook each separately.
2. Cook the rice normally and keep it ready.
3. Heat oil, fry onion until brown, then add the ground masala and fry.
4. Add the chopped tomato and cook until soft.
5. Add the cooked pulses/grains, salt, chilli powder, turmeric and coriander. Mix gently.
6. Add the cooked rice, mix carefully and steam briefly. Serve hot.
